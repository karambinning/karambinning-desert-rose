A note from the kitchen

The first shisha cafe in Luton — and we've kept the coals warm since.

Desert's Rose has poured Lebanese hospitality into a small corner of Park Street since 2004. The room is stone-walled and dimly lit; the grill runs from the afternoon through to the small hours; the shisha garden out back is for those who stay late and talk longer.

Everything we serve is halal. The kitchen leans on what we grew up with — chargrilled meats, generous meze, juices pressed fresh to order — and the welcome leans on the same Lebanese instinct: sit down, you'll be hungry.
